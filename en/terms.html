1. What PikeD is

PikeD is an app for predator anglers: water map, weather, air pressure, bite index, catch log, lure box, trips, planner and – optionally with an account – further features added over time. During the test phase PikeD is free. Test versions may contain bugs and can change at any time.

2. No guarantee

Water data comes from OpenStreetMap, weather from Open-Meteo. Bite index and water temperature are estimates. Closed seasons and minimum sizes are a guide without guarantee. Whether, where and what you may fish is governed solely by your permit, the water’s rules and the law. Watch the weather and your own safety at the water.

3. Account

An account is optional and only needed for cloud backup and crews. You are responsible for your access and do not share it. You must be at least 16 years old. You can delete your account in the app at any time (More → Account → Delete account).

4. Crew rules – zero tolerance for objectionable content

Crews are closed groups for anglers who respect each other. Before you start or join a crew, you agree to these rules. Not allowed in crews: insults, threats, harassment or hate; sexual, violent or otherwise objectionable content; animal cruelty or photos that show fish without respect; advertising, spam or fraud; revealing other people’s spots, names or photos without their permission; content that breaks laws or water rules.

You can report any catch and block any person. We review reported content within 24 hours. We remove violations; anyone who breaks the rules can be excluded from crews and from PikeD. The chief and admins of a crew can remove members.

5. Your content

What you enter in PikeD belongs to you. If you share something in a crew (e.g. photo, species, length, weight, water, planned fishing day), you allow us to show it to the members of that crew – only for this and only until you remove it from the crew, leave the crew or delete your account. Only share photos and content you have the rights to, and only show other people with their consent.
