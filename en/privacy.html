In short

PikeD stores everything you enter only on your phone. There is no advertising and no tracking. Your catches, trips, photos and fishing spots only go to our server if you use cloud backup with an account – privately, only for you. Only if you agree to “Anonymous statistics” are catches set to “Statistics” sent to our server – without spot, name or photos; likewise PikeD only counts which features you use if you agree. Only what you share yourself goes to a crew – never your spot. You don’t need an account (see “Account”).

What is stored on your phone

Catches (species, length, lure, time, weather, water and – if you allow it – the location), trips with the recorded route, your lure box with photos, saved and own waters with photos, spots, planned fishing days, licences with photos, your own closed-season rules and your settings.

For each catch you can choose a visibility (private or statistics). Catches are only shared if you agree to anonymous statistics (see there).

In your profile you can optionally enter your name, photo, year of birth and gender. These also stay on your phone; your name only appears on shared pictures if you switch that on. Your crews only see your profile photo if you allow it under More → Account (see “Crews”).

If you delete PikeD, all of this data is removed from the phone.

Permissions

Location: to show you on the map, recognise the nearest water, calculate weather, sun and moon times for your location and save where a fish was caught.

Background location (“Always”): only during a trip you start yourself – so the route is recorded even when the screen is locked. Recording stops with “End trip”. If you forget, PikeD reminds you after 5 hours and ends the trip by itself after 12 hours. Without this permission, recording only works while the app is open.

Camera and photos: for catch photos, lure photos, photos of licences and your profile photo.

Notifications: weather alerts and reminders (e.g. before a planned fishing day, to back up, before a licence expires or when a trip is still running) are scheduled directly on your phone – without a server. Only crew messages come via our server (see “Crew messages”).

All permissions are optional and can be changed at any time in your phone’s settings.

Services the app queries

For weather and maps PikeD queries the following services. The coordinates of your location, the place of a catch, a trip, a planned fishing day or the map area are transmitted, and for technical reasons your phone’s IP address. The weather service receives the coordinates rounded to about 1 km. Your catches, photos and trips themselves are not transmitted.

• Weather: Open-Meteo (Open-Meteo.com, Switzerland) – weather data licensed under CC BY 4.0

• Current: on rivers PikeD additionally requests the river discharge from Open-Meteo (GloFAS model of the Copernicus Emergency Management Service). Only the centres of grid cells (about 5 × 4 km) around the place are transmitted, not your exact location.

• Minimum version: at start-up PikeD asks our server (Supabase, Ireland – see “Account”) which app version is required at least. Only the IP address is transmitted; nothing about you is stored.

• Map: Apple Maps (Apple)

• Place search: Geoapify (KEPTAGO LTD, Paphos, Cyprus; servers in the EU) – when you press “Search” in the map search. The request goes through our server (Supabase, Ireland); Geoapify only receives your search text, not your IP address or location. Only if that fails: Nominatim of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom), then directly from your phone.

• Gauges: PEGELONLINE of the German Federal Waterways and Shipping Administration (ITZBund, Germany) – on large rivers and canals. Once a month PikeD downloads the list of all gauges, picks the matching gauge on your phone and then only requests the values of that gauge. Your location is not transmitted.

• Flood situation: Cross-state flood portal (run by the environmental agencies of the German states, incl. LUBW and the Bavarian Environment Agency) – on rivers PikeD downloads the flood levels of all gauges and picks the matching one on your phone. Your location is not transmitted.

• Water from satellite: for lakes of 3 hectares or more, PikeD asks our server (Supabase, Ireland) for clarity and algae. Only the lake’s identifier and its rectangle from OpenStreetMap are transmitted – not your location, no account. Our server fetches the values from the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (European Space Agency ESA / EU) and stores them per lake.

• Nearest place for waters without a name (“pond near …”): when you tap such a water, the iPhone asks Apple Maps for the nearest place (the location of the water is transmitted). Only if that returns nothing: Nominatim (see above).

• Waters: Overpass servers of FOSSGIS e. V. (Germany), OpenStreetMap France (France) and Private.coffee (Austria) – only for areas not included in the built-in water package. The map area (about 28 × 18 km) around the place is transmitted.

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app features you use). Switzerland and the United Kingdom are covered by adequacy decisions of the European Commission. Waters in the built-in package and the search for waters need no query.

Backup and sharing

PikeD creates a backup as a file on your phone. Where you save it (e.g. iCloud Drive) or whom you send it to is your choice via the phone’s share menu. We do not receive the file. Please note: it also contains the locations of your catches and the routes of your trips.

The year in review you can share as a picture contains no places and no names of waters.

Anonymous statistics (server at Supabase)

Only if you agree under More → Anonymous statistics does PikeD transmit catches set to “Statistics” (or “Public”) to our server. They are used for water statistics and a bite index that learns from many anglers’ catches.

What is transmitted: the water (for lakes, ponds and other still waters the OpenStreetMap ID, for rivers and canals only the name, plus the type of water – never the spot on the bank), date and hour, species, length, weight, lure (type, colour, length and weight – not its name), the weather at that time (air pressure and trend, wind, air and estimated water temperature, cloud cover, precipitation), moon brightness and the app version. Never transmitted: your name, your e-mail address, your exact location (spot), trip routes, photos and notes.

So that you can delete your data yourself, the app signs in to the server without name or password and receives a random number. This number does not say who you are and is not linked to your account. For technical reasons the server receives your phone’s IP address with every connection; it is not stored with your catches. We only show statistics for a water once at least 5 different anglers have reported there – and only in summary, never individual catches.

The server is provided by Supabase, Inc. (USA); the data is stored in a data centre in Ireland (EU). We have a data processing agreement with Supabase; the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses apply to any access from the USA.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time under More → Anonymous statistics – the app then deletes everything shared so far from the server (if you are offline, the next time you open it). If you set a catch to “Private” or delete it, it is also deleted from the server. “Delete my data from the server” removes everything at once. Otherwise we keep the data as long as PikeD exists.

Usage statistics: Only if you agree (one-time question or More → Usage statistics) does PikeD count on your phone which screens and features you open on a day (e.g. “Map: 3”, “Trip started: 1”) and send these numbers once a day, together with the type of phone and the random number, to our server. There they are only added up across all devices; we keep the random number for at most 15 days so that a day is not counted twice. Catches, lengths, weights, lures, spots, location, name and account are never counted. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time under More → Usage statistics; the app then also deletes all numbers not yet sent.

Account (optional)

Under More → Account you can create an account voluntarily – for cloud backup and crews. Without an account everything else in PikeD works as before.

You can sign in with Apple, with Google or with a code sent by e-mail. We store: a random account number, your e-mail address (with Apple, if you wish, a relay address created by Apple), the sign-in method, the times of sign-up and last use, and the nickname you choose. We only store your real name if you explicitly allow others to see it. There is no password. We send the e-mail sign-in code via our mailbox at IONOS SE, Montabaur (Germany). Your catches, trips, photos and spots only go into the cloud backup (see there) – and to your crews only what you share there yourself (never your spot).

When signing in with Apple or Google we only receive your e-mail address and – the first time – your name as a suggestion. The privacy policies of Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) or Google (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland) also apply.

The account is stored on our server at Supabase, Inc. (USA) in a data centre in Ireland (EU). We have a data processing agreement with Supabase; the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses apply to any access from the USA. We do not pass your data on.

The legal basis is providing the features you use with the account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). “Delete account” (More → Account) immediately deletes your account and everything linked to it from the server. Everything on your phone stays.

Cloud backup

If you are signed in with an account, PikeD automatically backs up your data to our server: once a day when you open the app and after a trip ends, on request only via Wi-Fi. You can also tap “Back up now” yourself.

The backup contains everything stored in PikeD – including photos, notes, the recorded routes of your trips and the exact locations of your catches and spots. It is kept in private storage that only your account can access; other users never see it. We do not look at it and do not pass it on.

We keep the last 3 backups; older ones are deleted automatically. A backup is only restored if you tap it yourself under More → Backup. The data is stored on our server at Supabase in Ireland (see “Account”).

The legal basis is providing the cloud backup you use with your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Under More → Backup you can delete all cloud backups; “Delete account” also deletes them from the server immediately.

Crews (closed groups)

With an account you can start crews or join them with an invite code. A crew has at most 20 members. The other members see your nickname and – only if you allowed it – your profile picture and your real name. For this the picture is reduced in size and stored without location data in private storage that only your crews can read.

If you share a catch in a crew, we transmit: photo (reduced in size, without location and camera data), species, length, weight, the name of the water and the day. Never transmitted: your spot, coordinates, the route of your trip, lure and notes. You choose when the crew sees the catch (now, after the trip or after 3, 7 or 14 days); until then nobody but you sees it. We also store who gave which catch a “Tight lines”, and for each shared catch and fishing day a random ID of your phone – so entries don’t overwrite each other if you use your account on two phones. It does not reveal which phone it is.

If you report a catch, we store who reported which catch and why, send ourselves the report by e-mail and review it within 24 hours. If you block someone, we store this so you no longer see their catches; the blocked person is not told.

If you give a water without a name your own name and share it with your crew (only if you confirm), we store for that crew the name, the OpenStreetMap ID of the water, your account number and the time. Members then see the water with this name and your nickname (“Name by …”) – never your spot.

If you show a planned fishing day in a crew, we only transmit the name of the water and the dates – never your spot, note or packing list. We also store who tapped “I’m in”.

What you share in a crew is only seen by the members of that crew – no other users and not the public. The data is stored on our server at Supabase in Ireland (see “Account”).

The legal basis is providing the crew feature you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can remove a shared catch from the crew or delete it at any time, leave the crew or – as chief – delete the crew; the shared data is then removed from the server. “Delete account” removes everything linked to your account.

Crew messages (push)

If you are in a crew with an account and allow notifications, you get a short message as soon as someone in your crew shares a catch (e.g. “Kreso shared a catch: 92 cm”). For this, your phone registers a device address (push token) with our server. We store this token, your account, the type of phone and the app language.

Our server sends the messages via Apple’s notification service (Apple Push Notification Service, Apple Inc., USA). Only the token and the text of the message are transmitted: the crew name, the nickname of the person sharing and the length or weight. Photos, waters, spots or locations are never transmitted. For possible access from the USA, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses apply.

The legal basis is providing the crew feature you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can switch off crew messages at any time under More → Notifications; the token is then deleted from the server immediately – likewise when you sign out and with “Delete account”. Tokens that Apple reports as invalid are deleted automatically.

Feedback by e-mail

“Feedback & report a bug” opens your own mail app with a message to info@piked.app. It already contains the app version, system version and language so we can trace errors. Only what you send yourself is sent.

We process your e-mail address and message to handle your feedback (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in a bug-free app). Our mailboxes are hosted by IONOS SE, Montabaur, Germany. We delete messages no later than 6 months after the end of the test.

Crash and error reports (Sentry)

So we can find and fix bugs, PikeD automatically reports crashes and errors to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA). We chose data storage in the EU (Frankfurt).

Only technical information is sent: the error message and where it occurred in the code, app version, device model, system version, language and time. Not sent: locations (coordinates are removed beforehand), catches, trips, photos, names, the IP address or screenshots.

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a stable, bug-free app). A data processing agreement is in place with Sentry; transfers to the USA are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Reports are deleted after 90 days at the latest.

Test version via TestFlight

You receive the test version via TestFlight (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland). Apple may pass on crash reports, technical device information and feedback you send there. Apple’s privacy policy also applies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Just write to info@piked.app. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Baden-Württemberg or the authority where you live.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, available website). Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
