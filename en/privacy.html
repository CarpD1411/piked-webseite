In short

PikeD stores everything you enter only on your phone. There is no advertising and no tracking. Your catches, trips, photos and fishing spots only go to our server if you use cloud backup with an account – privately, only for you. Only if you agree to “Anonymous statistics” are catches set to “Statistics” sent to our server – without spot, name or photos. You don’t need an account (see “Account”).

What is stored on your phone

Catches (species, length, lure, time, weather, water and – if you allow it – the location), trips with the recorded route, your lure box with photos, saved and own waters with photos, spots, planned fishing days, licences with photos, your own closed-season rules and your settings.

For each catch you can choose a visibility (private, statistics, public). Catches are only shared if you agree to anonymous statistics (see there).

In your profile you can optionally enter your name, photo, year of birth and gender. These also stay on your phone; your name only appears on shared pictures if you switch that on.

If you delete PikeD, all of this data is removed from the phone.

Permissions

Location: to show you on the map, recognise the nearest water, calculate weather, sun and moon times for your location and save where a fish was caught.

Background location (“Always”): only during a trip you start yourself – so the route is recorded even when the screen is locked. Recording stops with “End trip”. Without this permission, recording only works while the app is open.

Camera and photos: for catch photos, lure photos, photos of licences and your profile photo.

Notifications: weather alerts and reminders (e.g. before a planned fishing day, to back up or before a licence expires) are scheduled directly on your phone – without a server.

All permissions are optional and can be changed at any time in your phone’s settings.

Services the app queries

For weather and maps PikeD queries the following services. The coordinates of your location, the place of a catch, a trip, a planned fishing day or the map area are transmitted, and for technical reasons your phone’s IP address. Your catches, photos and trips themselves are not transmitted.

• Weather: Open-Meteo (Open-Meteo.com, Switzerland)

• Map: Apple Maps (Apple)

• Place search: Nominatim of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom) – when you press “Search” in the map search (your search text is transmitted) and when you tap a water without a name (its location is transmitted to find the nearest place).

• Waters: Overpass servers of FOSSGIS e. V. (Germany), OpenStreetMap France (France) and Private.coffee (Austria) – only for areas not included in the built-in water package. The map area (about 28 × 18 km) around the place is transmitted.

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app features you use). Switzerland and the United Kingdom are covered by adequacy decisions of the European Commission. Waters in the built-in package and the search for waters need no query.

Backup and sharing

PikeD creates a backup as a file on your phone. Where you save it (e.g. iCloud Drive) or whom you send it to is your choice via the phone’s share menu. We do not receive the file. Please note: it also contains the locations of your catches and the routes of your trips.

The year in review you can share as a picture contains no places and no names of waters.

Anonymous statistics (server at Supabase)

Only if you agree under More → Anonymous statistics does PikeD transmit catches set to “Statistics” (or “Public”) to our server. They are used for water statistics and a bite index that learns from many anglers’ catches.

What is transmitted: the water (OpenStreetMap ID and type of water – not the spot on the bank), date and hour, species, length, weight, lure (type, colour, length and weight – not its name), the weather at that time (air pressure and trend, wind, air and estimated water temperature, cloud cover, precipitation), moon brightness and the app version. Never transmitted: your name, your e-mail address, your exact location (spot), trip routes, photos and notes.

So that you can delete your data yourself, the app signs in to the server without name or password and receives a random number. This number does not say who you are and is not linked to your account. For technical reasons the server receives your phone’s IP address with every connection; it is not stored with your catches. We only show statistics for a water once at least 5 different anglers have reported there – and only in summary, never individual catches.

The server is provided by Supabase, Inc. (USA); the data is stored in a data centre in Ireland (EU). We have a data processing agreement with Supabase; the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses apply to any access from the USA.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time under More → Anonymous statistics – the app then deletes everything shared so far from the server (if you are offline, the next time you open it). If you set a catch to “Private” or delete it, it is also deleted from the server. “Delete my data from the server” removes everything at once. Otherwise we keep the data as long as PikeD exists.

Account (optional)

Under More → Account you can create an account voluntarily – for cloud backup and features coming later (e.g. crew). Without an account everything else in PikeD works as before.

You can sign in with Apple, with Google or with a code sent by e-mail. We store: a random account number, your e-mail address (with Apple, if you wish, a relay address created by Apple), the sign-in method, the times of sign-up and last use, and the nickname you choose. We only store your real name if you explicitly allow others to see it. There is no password. Your catches, trips, photos and spots only go into the cloud backup (see there).

When signing in with Apple or Google we only receive your e-mail address and – the first time – your name as a suggestion. The privacy policies of Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) or Google (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland) also apply.

The account is stored on our server at Supabase, Inc. (USA) in a data centre in Ireland (EU). We have a data processing agreement with Supabase; the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses apply to any access from the USA. We do not pass your data on.

The legal basis is providing the features you use with the account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). “Delete account” (More → Account) immediately deletes your account and everything linked to it from the server. Everything on your phone stays.

Cloud backup

If you are signed in with an account, PikeD automatically backs up your data to our server: once a day when you open the app and after a trip ends, on request only via Wi-Fi. You can also tap “Back up now” yourself.

The backup contains everything stored in PikeD – including photos, notes, the recorded routes of your trips and the exact locations of your catches and spots. It is kept in private storage that only your account can access; other users never see it. We do not look at it and do not pass it on.

We keep the last 3 backups; older ones are deleted automatically. A backup is only restored if you tap it yourself under More → Backup. The data is stored on our server at Supabase in Ireland (see “Account”).

The legal basis is providing the cloud backup you use with your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Under More → Backup you can delete all cloud backups; “Delete account” also deletes them from the server immediately.

Feedback by e-mail

“Feedback & report a bug” opens your own mail app with a message to info@piked.app. It already contains the app version, system version and language so we can trace errors. Only what you send yourself is sent.

We process your e-mail address and message to handle your feedback (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in a bug-free app). Our mailboxes are hosted by IONOS SE, Montabaur, Germany. We delete messages no later than 6 months after the end of the test.

Crash and error reports (Sentry)

So we can find and fix bugs, PikeD automatically reports crashes and errors to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA). We chose data storage in the EU (Frankfurt).

Only technical information is sent: the error message and where it occurred in the code, app version, device model, system version, language and time. Not sent: locations (coordinates are removed beforehand), catches, trips, photos, names, the IP address or screenshots.

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a stable, bug-free app). A data processing agreement is in place with Sentry; transfers to the USA are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Reports are deleted after 90 days at the latest.

Test version via TestFlight

You receive the test version via TestFlight (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland). Apple may pass on crash reports, technical device information and feedback you send there. Apple’s privacy policy also applies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Just write to info@piked.app. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Baden-Württemberg or the authority where you live.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics or advertising services and loads no content from third-party servers (not even fonts).

It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub necessarily processes your IP address and browser information to deliver the page and protect it against attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, available website). Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
